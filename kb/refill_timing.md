# Refill timing rules

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

Insurance plans reject a refill as "refill too soon" (rejection code 79) when too little of the current supply has been used. For non-controlled medications the plan pays once 75 percent of the days' supply is used; for controlled substances it is 90 percent.

The eligible date is counted from the last fill date: 75 percent of a 30-day supply is reached on day 23, so a 30-day fill picked up on September 21 is covered from October 14. For a 90-day supply it is day 68.

A claim rejected as refill too soon can be rerun once the eligible date has passed; no new prescription is needed while refills remain.

Converting to a 90-day supply is processed as a new claim and is not blocked by the refill-too-soon window when the prescription has refills remaining.

Early refills for travel ("vacation supply") need a one-time override that the pharmacist requests from the plan; agents should not promise an override.

A refill submitted during pharmacy hours is usually ready about 2 hours later at the pickup store; one submitted within 2 hours of closing is ready the next morning. Filled prescriptions are held for pickup for 7 days. A Walgreens prescription can be refilled for pickup at any Walgreens store; no transfer is needed.
