# Refill timing rules

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

Insurance plans reject a refill as "refill too soon" (rejection code 79) when too little of the current supply has been used. For non-controlled medications the plan pays once about 75 percent of the days' supply is used; for controlled substances it is 90 percent.

The eligible date is counted from the last fill date, so a 30-day fill picked up on September 21 becomes eligible on October 24.

Early refills for travel ("vacation supply") need a one-time override that the pharmacist requests from the plan; agents should not promise an override.

Converting to a 90-day supply is processed as a new claim and is not blocked by the refill-too-soon window when the prescription has refills remaining.
