# Prescription transfers

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

Prescriptions move between Walgreens stores instantly; the receiving store can usually fill within 15 minutes of the request.

Transfers from another pharmacy chain take 1 to 2 business days because the pharmacist must contact the other pharmacy.

Controlled substances in Schedule II cannot be transferred and need a new prescription; Schedules III to V can be transferred once.

To start a transfer, confirm the receiving store, the prescription number or medication name, and the current pharmacy's phone number.
