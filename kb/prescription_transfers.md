# Prescription transfers

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

A transfer moves a prescription from another pharmacy chain (for example CVS) to a Walgreens store. Walgreens stores share one prescription record, so picking up a Walgreens prescription at a different Walgreens store is a refill at that store, not a transfer.

To start a transfer, confirm the receiving Walgreens store, the medication name and strength, and the current pharmacy's name, location and phone number. The other pharmacy's prescription number speeds it up but is not required. It is used only for the transfer and never verifies identity.

Transfers from another pharmacy chain take 1 to 2 business days because the Walgreens pharmacist must contact the other pharmacy. Give the expected date from the transfer request; the store texts or calls when it is ready.

Controlled substances in Schedule II cannot be transferred and need a new prescription; Schedules III to V can be transferred once.
