# Cost and savings options

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

A 90-day supply usually carries a lower combined copay than three 30-day fills; quote both from the insurance check before offering. A 90-day conversion uses the remaining refills on the prescription, so no new prescription is needed while refills remain.

Manufacturer coupons cannot be combined with federal plans such as Medicare Part D or Medicaid. A discount card can be used instead of insurance on a given fill, but the two cannot be stacked on the same fill.

Generic substitution is automatic unless the prescriber marked "dispense as written".

Agents may quote prices from the insurance check only, never from memory.
