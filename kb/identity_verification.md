# Caller identity verification

<!-- Mock demo policy, not real Walgreens policy. -->

Before any prescription, claim or health detail is discussed, verify the caller with two identifiers from the patient record (full name and date of birth) plus the phone number on file. Ask the caller to state each one; never read the record's values to them for a yes or no. Then send a one-time passcode to the phone on file and have the caller read it back.

A prescription number from another pharmacy (for example a CVS Rx number) is not on the Walgreens record and never verifies identity. It is used only to request a transfer, after the caller is verified; the drug name and the other pharmacy's location are enough when the caller does not have it.

If the caller is not the patient, they must be listed as an authorized representative on the record; otherwise offer to call the patient directly.

Never confirm or deny that a prescription exists before verification is complete. Agents may say that they are verifying identity for privacy reasons.
